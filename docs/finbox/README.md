## Summary

### 1. [Hiring Manager Round](01-hm-round.md)

An HM is deciding whether to hand you a platform and stop worrying about it — a different
screen from the Zamp CEO round, so [six different questions](01-hm-round.md#a-what-this-round-is):
are you actually a DE, can you own it end-to-end, how big are the stack gaps and will you
bluff, will you raise the team, will you stay, what do you do in month one.
[FinBox in the terms the conversation will use](01-hm-round.md#b-finbox-in-the-terms-the-conversation-will-use)
— products, customers, what the platform is for, tenancy at bank grade — then the
[JD line by line](01-hm-round.md#c-the-jd-decoded--evidence-map) marked strong / bridge / gap
against the résumé.

Worked answers: [tell me about yourself, DE cut](01-hm-round.md#d-tell-me-about-yourself--the-de-cut),
[the platform deep dive](01-hm-round.md#e-the-platform-deep-dive) with the numbers to fill in,
[why leaving Stripe](01-hm-round.md#f-why-are-you-leaving-stripe) cut to 50 seconds for a
manager, [the break](01-hm-round.md#g-the-break) (five months, dbgit as the artefact),
[why FinBox](01-hm-round.md#h-what-are-you-looking-for--why-finbox). Then the
[lead-level stories](01-hm-round.md#i-lead-level-stories) the Zamp bank lacks — a worked
[mentoring arc](01-hm-round.md#the-mentoring-story) (four engineers in, two became execution
partners) and [planning story](01-hm-round.md#the-planning-story), plus standards and failure — [three-beat answers to every "have you used X"](01-hm-round.md#j-the-gap-questions)
— ClickHouse, dbt, Terraform, Glue/MSK/DMS, Debezium, Delta, Data Vault —
a [first-90-days plan](01-hm-round.md#k-first-90-days),
[questions to ask](01-hm-round.md#l-questions-to-ask-the-hm) and the
[gaps to close first](01-hm-round.md#m-gaps-to-close-before-the-round).
